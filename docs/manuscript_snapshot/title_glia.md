# Title

Animal-level heterogeneity in interferon-responsive transcription after sevoflurane anesthesia and laparotomy in aged hippocampal microglia

## Running title

Microglial interferon heterogeneity

## Main Points

- Aged hippocampal microglia showed heterogeneous transcription after sevoflurane anesthesia plus laparotomy.
- Interferon-related estimates depended on animal inclusion and analysis method; no reference partition expanded consistently.
